SERVICES · CUSTOMS COORDINATION

Customs coordination that keeps cargo moving

Documentation and clearance managed alongside licensed customs agents, so a shipment isn't held up at the border waiting on paperwork.

What's included

Clearance is usually where a shipment's timeline slips — a missing document, a misclassified tariff line, or an inspection nobody was expecting. We manage the coordination around it so those delays are caught early rather than discovered at the border.

  • Pre-clearance document review before cargo arrives at the border
  • Tariff classification and duty computation checked against current schedules
  • Coordination with licensed customs agents and border authorities
  • Support arranged for physical examination when a consignment is flagged for inspection
  • Clearance status reported back through the same shipment record used for tracking

Why it's handled this way

We work alongside licensed customs agencies rather than duplicating their role — our part is making sure the paperwork they need is ready before the cargo arrives, and that a clearance issue gets flagged to the client immediately, not after it's already caused a delay.

Because clearance status runs through the same visibility platform as the rest of a shipment, a client isn't left checking a separate system to find out where customs stands.
