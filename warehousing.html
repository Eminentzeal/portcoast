SERVICES · WAREHOUSING

Warehousing and distribution, close to the port

Storage and inland distribution positioned near port and border crossings, so cargo doesn't sit idle waiting for the next leg of its journey.

What's included

Warehousing is where cargo waits between the port and its final destination — and where it can pick up damage, delay or confusion if it isn't managed properly. We treat it as an active part of the shipment, not just storage space.

  • Short and long-term storage with inventory recorded against each consignment
  • Cross-docking for cargo that only needs to change transport mode, not sit in storage
  • Inventory management through our warehouse management system (WMS)
  • Pick, pack and onward distribution to inland destinations
  • Condition checks on receipt and dispatch, logged against the shipment record

Why location matters

Our facilities are positioned for quick access to the ports and border crossings our clients use most, which keeps the gap between "cleared customs" and "in the warehouse" as short as possible.

Every pallet or container is entered into the same system used for shipment visibility, so a client checking on a container mid-voyage and a client checking on stock in the warehouse are looking at the same kind of record.
