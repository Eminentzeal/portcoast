SERVICES · PROCUREMENT & SUPPLY

Procurement and supply, sourced and delivered

We source, manage and deliver the materials and supplies our clients need — from marine stores for a vessel at berth to general supplies for an ongoing operation.

What's included

Procurement runs alongside our logistics work, but it's used on its own too — clients bring us a sourcing need and we manage it from vendor selection through to delivery.

  • Vendor sourcing and evaluation against price, quality and delivery timelines
  • Marine and ship stores procurement for vessels calling at port
  • Industrial and general supplies for ongoing operational needs
  • Purchase order management and delivery tracking through to sign-off
  • Quality checks on goods before they leave for final delivery

How we manage vendors

We maintain relationships with vendors across the categories we source for regularly, which means most requests can be quoted quickly rather than started from scratch. Where a client needs something outside our existing network, we evaluate new suppliers against the same standard before anything is ordered.

Every order is tracked from confirmation to delivery, with the same reporting and invoicing used across our other services — so procurement spend shows up in the same place as freight and warehousing costs.
