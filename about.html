ABOUT US

Built around the port, not just the paperwork

Portcoast Africa Limited is a wholly Nigerian-owned logistics and supply chain company built for the realities of moving goods in and out of African ports — where congestion, documentation delays, and gaps between agents can cost you time and money.

Nigerian-owned West Africa reach 20+ years combined experience 4 transport modes In-house TMS & WMS

Who we are

Portcoast Africa Limited was established in 2024 as a wholly Nigerian-owned limited liability company, founded on a simple premise: that the people moving goods through West African ports deserve one point of contact — not a chain of separate agents who hand off responsibility and lose accountability at every step.

We operate at the intersection of shipping, procurement and inland transport. That means coordinating vessels, cargo handling, warehousing and last-mile delivery as a single, connected operation — managed from our base in Abuja with direct relationships at key West African ports.

Our team brings together decades of combined experience in ship handling, supply chain management and port operations across the region. We understand the practical realities: documentation that stalls at customs, berths that fill up unexpectedly, and the phone calls it takes to get a straight answer about where a shipment actually is. We built Portcoast to fix that.

How we're organised

Rather than separate teams that hand a shipment between them, our ship handling, procurement and warehousing staff work from the same shipment record. When a vessel needs stores, or a container needs inland transport after customs clearance, it gets arranged internally — no waiting on a third-party agent to be briefed and brought in.

Every client account is managed by a single point of contact who can speak to every part of the journey — from origin to signature. That's not a marketing promise; it's the practical result of how our operation is structured.

What we offer

  • Ship handling — Berth coordination, vessel agency and cargo operations at port.
  • Procurement & supply — Sourcing and vendor management aligned to your operating needs.
  • Multimodal transport — Road, rail, sea and air moves planned as one route.
  • Warehousing & distribution — Storage and last-mile handoff close to port and border crossings.
  • In-house TMS & WMS — Real-time shipment and inventory visibility, shared with clients.
