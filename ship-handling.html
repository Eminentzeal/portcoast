SERVICES · SHIP HANDLING

Ship handling, run from berth to departure

We act as the point of contact between a vessel and everyone it needs to deal with at port — coordinating arrival, cargo operations and clearance so turnaround stays on schedule.

What's included

Ship handling covers everything a vessel needs once it's within reach of port — before it arrives, while it's alongside, and until it's cleared to leave. We manage this as vessel agents, working directly with port authorities, terminal operators and cargo interests on the client's behalf.

  • Pre-arrival notice, berth application and scheduling with port authorities
  • Vessel agency for the duration of the port call, including crew and stores coordination
  • Cargo operations oversight — loading, discharge, and tally against shipping documents
  • Port disbursement accounts, prepared and reconciled after departure
  • Liaison with surveyors, stevedores and terminal staff on operational issues as they arise

Why it's handled this way

A vessel at port is dealing with several parties at once — the terminal, customs, surveyors, suppliers — each with their own paperwork and timeline. We take that coordination on directly, so the shipowner or charterer has one desk to call rather than five.

Because our procurement and warehousing teams sit alongside ship handling, a vessel that also needs stores, spares or onward inland transport for its cargo can have that arranged without bringing in a separate agent.
