SERVICES · REPORTING & INVOICING

Reporting and invoicing clients can reconcile

Line-level invoices and shipment reporting, so finance and operations are looking at the same numbers at the same time.

What's included

A shipment isn't finished when it's delivered — it's finished when the numbers behind it are accounted for. We treat reporting and invoicing as part of the service, not paperwork tacked on afterward.

  • Line-level invoicing broken out by shipment, service and cost component
  • Recurring reporting on shipment volumes, costs and turnaround times
  • Reconciliation support against purchase orders and existing contracts
  • Reports delivered on a schedule that fits how the client's finance team works
  • The same figures available through the shipment record used for visibility tracking

Why it's handled this way

Invoices that don't break down by shipment or cost component are hard to reconcile against a purchase order or a budget — so we itemize by default rather than sending a single bundled figure.

Because reporting draws from the same shipment record as tracking, the numbers finance receives match what operations were seeing throughout the shipment, rather than being reconstructed separately at the end.
