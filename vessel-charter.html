SERVICES · VESSEL CHARTER

Vessel charter, matched to the cargo

We arrange charter for bulk, break-bulk and project cargo, matching vessel type, tonnage and schedule to what's actually being moved rather than whatever happens to be available.

What's included

Chartering is arranged as a service in its own right, not just an add-on to a port call — clients bring us a cargo and a route, and we work out the vessel and terms to move it.

  • Vessel sourcing for bulk, break-bulk and project cargo, matched to tonnage and hold configuration
  • Voyage and time charter arrangements, depending on how the client's schedule is structured
  • Charter party terms reviewed and negotiated on the client's behalf
  • Laycan planning coordinated with port and berth availability
  • Handover into our ship handling and cargo operations once the vessel is fixed

Why it's handled this way

Chartering decisions made without visibility into what happens at the port on either end tend to cost money later — a vessel fixed on rate alone can still sit waiting on a berth it wasn't scheduled against. We plan charter and port call together, so the fixture matches what's actually achievable at both ends.

Because ship handling sits alongside chartering, a vessel we've fixed moves straight into berth coordination and cargo operations without changing hands to a separate agent.
